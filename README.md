# Deekinge Revenue Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project is a Power BI Revenue Analytics Dashboard developed to analyze business performance through revenue, cost, gross profit, product sales, country performance, and regional distribution.

The dashboard transforms raw business data into actionable insights using Power Query for data preparation and Power BI for data modeling, visualization, KPI reporting, and analytical storytelling.

---

# Objectives

- Analyze overall revenue and profitability performance.
- Evaluate gross profit contribution across countries and regions.
- Identify high-performing products and markets.
- Compare sales volume against revenue generation.
- Monitor monthly revenue and profit trends.
- Generate recommendations to improve business performance.

# Key Insights

1. The business generated $251.54M revenue with $125.41M gross profit, resulting in approximately 50% gross margin.

2. The United States contributed the highest quantity sold and gross profit, showing strong volume-to-profit performance.

3. North America generated the largest revenue contribution compared with other continents.

4. S-Works TT Helmet was the highest revenue-generating product, outperforming other products.

5. Revenue and profit declined significantly between June and July, highlighting a period requiring further investigation.

---

# Recommendations

1. Monitor major cost drivers to protect profitability margins.

2. Analyze successful strategies in high-performing regions and apply them to weaker markets.

3. Prioritize high-performing products while reviewing lower-revenue products.

4. Investigate seasonal factors behind monthly revenue fluctuations.

5. Develop strategies to improve demand consistency throughout the year.
